Conference Registration

Registration for INDIS 2026 – International Conference on Design and Innovation Studies will be available soon. All participants, including authors of accepted papers and attendees without submissions, must complete the online registration process.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register under the Author category for the paper to be included in the conference programme and proceedings.

Registration Categories

Participants may register under one of the following categories:

Author Registration

Applicable to participants who have accepted papers in the conference.

Listener / Attendee Registration

Applicable to participants who wish to attend the conference without presenting a paper.

Within each category, participants may register as:

  • Student
  • Academician / Researcher
  • Industry Professional

What the Registration Includes

Conference registration provides access to:

  • All conference sessions and research presentations
  • Keynote lectures and panel discussions
  • Participation in Asia Design Week activities
  • Conference kit and materials
  • Tea / coffee breaks
  • Lunch for two conference days
  • Conference Gala Dinner
  • Certificate of participation
  • Inclusion of accepted and presented papers in the conference proceedings (for registered authors)

Author Registration Policy

  • At least one author must complete Registration for each accepted paper.
  • The registered author must present the paper during the conference.
  • Only registered and presented papers will be included in the conference proceedings and archival publication.

Registration Process

Participants will be able to register by:

  1. Filling the online registration form
  2. Selecting the appropriate registration category
  3. Completing payment of the registration fee
  4. Receiving a confirmation email from the conference secretariat

The registration and payment link will be updated soon on this page.

Important Notes

  • Registration fees may increase across phases (Early / Regular / Late).
  • Registration fees do not include accommodation or travel.
  • All participants must complete registration before attending the conference.

Accommodation

Accommodation is not included in the conference registration fee.

A limited number of rooms may be available at the IIT Guwahati Guest House. Participants interested in staying on campus may request accommodation during registration.

Please note:

  • Guest house accommodation is subject to availability
  • Rooms will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis
  • Payment for accommodation must be made directly by the participant

Participants who are unable to secure accommodation on campus are encouraged to arrange their own stay in nearby hotels.

The conference organizing team will share a list of recommended hotels near IIT Guwahati to assist participants in planning their stay.
